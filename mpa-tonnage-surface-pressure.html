MPa, tonnage and cake pressure: how to read hydraulic oil press specs

2026-11-07

Spec sheets for hydraulic oil presses often show the same pressure, 60 MPa, on every model. Buyers then assume all the models press equally hard. They do not.

Three different numbers

  • System pressure (MPa) is the oil pressure in the hydraulic circuit. On these presses it is about 60 MPa for every model.
  • Press force (tons) is system pressure multiplied by the piston area. A bigger piston gives more tons at the same 60 MPa.
  • Cake surface pressure is that force spread over the barrel area. This is what the seed actually feels.

Worked example

A 260 mm piston at 60 MPa gives about 324 tons of force, which is why the Model 325 is called a 325-ton press.
