Filtering pressed oil: why temperature decides your filter speed

2026-11-07

Freshly pressed oil carries fine solids. How clean it needs to be depends on where you sell it.

How clean is clean enough?

  • Walk-in shop: customers often want to see fresh crude oil; light settling may be enough.
  • Bottled oil for supermarkets: the oil must stay clear on the shelf, so proper filtration is required.
  • Export or refining: filtration is the first step before further processing.

The pneumatic paper-leaf filter

A common choice after a hydraulic press is a pneumatic filter with multi-layer filter paper, around 10 m² of filter area in stainless steel (SUS304). Compressed air pushes the oil through the paper. The paper is a consumable and is replaced as it fills.

The temperature rule

Filter the oil at about 60–70°C. Below 60°C the oil thickens and the filtering rate roughly halves, so a filter that seems too slow is often just filtering cold oil.
