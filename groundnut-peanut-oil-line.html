Groundnut (peanut) oil with a hydraulic press: line steps and machine count

2026-11-07

Groundnut oil can be hot pressed for a roasted aroma or cold pressed for a lighter oil. Unlike sesame, groundnut is crushed before pressing.

Hot-press route

  1. Clean, and shell if you buy in-shell nuts.
  2. Crush with a pre-press crusher.
  3. Roast the crushed material.
  4. Press in a hydraulic press.
  5. Filter, store, and bottle if needed.

The cold route is the same without roasting, and usually uses the heavier cold-press models.

Worked example

One published layout balances one pre-press crusher at about 500 kg of groundnut per hour with five roasting woks and five Model 325 presses. Each press uses a 390 mm by 800 mm barrel holding about 100 kg of crushed groundnut, and two barrels take about 1.5 hours including loading and unloading.

Treat this as a starting point. Your nut variety, moisture and working hours change the numbers, so ask for a test press with your own groundnuts before you buy.
