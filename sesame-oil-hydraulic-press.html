Making toasted sesame oil with a hydraulic press: the practical steps

2026-11-07

Toasted sesame oil is the classic product for a hydraulic oil press. The flavour comes from roasting, and the slow press keeps the oil clean.

The route

  1. Screen and clean the sesame to remove stones, straw and dust. Wash and dry only if the seed needs it.
  2. Roast the seed to the colour and aroma you want.
  3. Press hot in the hydraulic press. Sesame goes straight from the roaster into the press; it does not need a pre-press crusher.
  4. Filter the oil through a pneumatic paper filter.
  5. Store and bottle.

How much per load

In the 300 mm barrel used for hot pressing sesame, one load is about 30 kg of roasted seed.

Which press

For a shop selling fresh oil, a compact Korean-type press is usually enough. For a sesame oil brand or factory, the 300 / 325 hot-press models are the standard choice.

Filtration matters more than people expect

Filter the oil warm, at about 60–70°C. Below 60°C the oil thickens and the filtering rate roughly halves.
