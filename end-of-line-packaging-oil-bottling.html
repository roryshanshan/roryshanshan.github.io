After filling: the packing machines a bottled-oil line needs

2026-11-07

Filling and capping get all the attention, but a bottled-oil line also has to box, seal, stack and wrap the product. That end-of-line section decides how fast the whole line really runs.

The usual sequence

  1. Case erecting: forms and bottom-seals the empty carton.
  2. Case packing: puts the bottles into the carton.
  3. Carton sealing: folds the flaps and tapes the carton.
  4. Shrink wrapping (optional): film multipacks for supermarkets.
  5. Strapping (optional): straps cartons or pallets.
  6. Palletizing: stacks cartons on the pallet.
  7. Pallet wrapping: stretch film for transport.

What a small brand can skip

A small bottling room can pack by hand and use a semi-automatic case erector and sealer. Palletizers and in-line pallet wrappers only pay off on export or high-volume lines.
