Daily checks on a hydraulic oil press: gauges, relief valve and separator plates

2026-11-07

A hydraulic oil press has few moving parts, so most problems show up in the same few places. A two-minute check each morning catches them.

Compare the two pressure gauges

The press has two gauges: one on the pump and one on the cylinder. Their needles should always read the same. If they drift apart, one gauge is failing or something in the circuit is wrong. It is a free daily diagnostic.

Know your pressure limits

On the electric-contact gauges, the pump stops at 60 MPa. These settings and the high/low relief valves are set at the factory and should not be adjusted by operators.

Check the relief valve

Pressure is limited by a mechanical high/low-pressure relief valve. It is not electric, so check that it relieves at the right pressure rather than looking for a wiring fault.

Inspect the separator plates

The cake layers are separated by carbon-steel plates, not cloths. They are reusable hardware: look for wear, scoring and warping.

Keep the hydraulic oil right

Use the correct grade, keep the tank at the right level and change it on schedule. Guide: which hydraulic oil to use in an oil press.
