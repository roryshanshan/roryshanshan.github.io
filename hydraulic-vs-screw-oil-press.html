Hydraulic or screw oil press: which one fits your oil business?

2026-11-07

Most buyers compare the two machines on price per ton. That is the wrong starting point. The right question is what oil you sell and to whom.

How each machine works

A screw expeller feeds seed continuously through a rotating screw and squeezes oil out along the barrel. It runs all day and handles large tonnage cheaply.

A hydraulic oil press loads prepared seed into a barrel, closes it and presses it with a hydraulic cylinder. It works in batches: load, press, release, unload. Output per hour is lower, but the pressing is slow and even.

When the hydraulic press wins

  • You sell premium pressed oil, such as toasted sesame oil, walnut oil or cold-pressed specialty oils, where flavour and purity carry the price.
  • You run a shop or small brand and press a few hundred kilograms a day.
  • Your raw material is expensive, so you care more about oil quality than about throughput.

When the screw expeller wins

  • You sell bulk cooking oil to traders and compete on price.
  • You process several tons a day of common seed such as groundnut, sunflower or rapeseed.
  • You want continuous feeding with minimum labour per ton.

A common mistake

Buying a hydraulic press for a bulk-oil business, or buying a large screw line for a shop that sells fresh-pressed sesame oil by the bottle. Decide the product and the customer first, then pick the press.
