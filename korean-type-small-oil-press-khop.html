Small hydraulic oil presses for shops: KHOP-10, 15 and 20 compared

2026-11-07

Fresh-pressed oil shops need a press that fits in a store, runs on local power and makes oil in front of the customer. The Korean-type KHOP series is built for that.

KHOP-20KG1230 × 1120 × 16301800 kg380 V, 2.2–4 kW2.1 t / 24 h

The KHOP-15 is the most popular size. All three are mainly used for sesame, and also for walnut kernel, tea seed, pine nut and almond.

Shop or factory?

If you sell by the bottle over the counter, a KHOP is usually right. If you supply other shops or supermarkets, look at the 300 / 325 models instead.
